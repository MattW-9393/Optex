📘 User Guide – OptEx Workout Builder

OptEx is a simple, offline-friendly workout builder that helps you create, save, and reuse personalized training templates. No login. No fuss.

🚀 Getting Started

  1. Enter your exercise name (e.g., Push-ups, Squats).
  2. Input the number of reps (1–20) and sets (1–10).
  3. Click the Add button to log it in the workout table.
Example: Push-ups • 3 sets • 10 reps ➜ Click Add ➜ Added to table!

✨ Edit or Delete Exercises

  • Edit – (Coming soon!) Will allow updates to any row.
  • Delete – Instantly removes that exercise from the workout list.

💾 Save as Workout Template

  1. Enter a template name in the save section (e.g., "Leg Day").
  2. Click Save Workout.
  3. Your workout is stored in your browser for reuse.

Saving clears the current table and resets your session for a new workout.

🧹 Clear Current Workout

Click the "Clear Current Workout" button to remove all exercises from the session. This will not affect saved templates.

📂 Load Saved Templates

Templates appear under the "My Templates" section as cards.

  • Open Workout – (Coming soon!) Will repopulate your workout from the template.
  • Close – Hides the template card.

💡 Tips & Notes

  • Your data is stored locally in your browser — no internet required.
  • Saved workouts persist across sessions.
  • Only the current workout is cleared when saving or using "Clear Workout."

⚠️ Known Limitations

  • Edit functionality is not yet live.
  • Open Workout from template is in development.
  • Templates cannot yet be deleted or renamed.

Thanks for using OptEx! 💪
